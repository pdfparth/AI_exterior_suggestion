1Upload

Four numbered stages down one page. The dropzone accepts a drag or a click, and two sample photos are offered so the flow can be demonstrated without hunting for a file. A status line reports the quality gate as it runs.

2Detected surfaces

The photo is overlaid with one coloured polygon per detected surface, beside a checklist carrying each surface's confidence. Colour links a row to its shape; hovering a row highlights the polygon. Unticking a row excludes that surface from the estimate.

3Real-world scale and surface list

The scale panel states which reference object was used, its assumed size and the model's confidence, then exposes the two facade dimensions as editable fields. These multiply every cost in the system, so they are shown rather than buried. Warnings that affect accuracy sit directly below the list.

4Material selection

Materials are grouped by component, each a labelled chip with a colour swatch. Selecting one applies it to every surface of that component and recalculates the estimate immediately. "Leave as is" drops the component from both the render and the cost.

5Redesign and comparison

A plain side-by-side, original on the left and redesign on the right. Here Gemini was rate-limited, so the app fell back to the local compositor and said so — note that the windows stay untouched while the walls take the sand texture, and the original lighting survives the repaint.

6Cost estimate

Headline totals only — grand total, material and labour. The per-line areas, quantities, wastage and assumptions deliberately live in the PDF report rather than on screen, so this view stays readable and the document that reaches a contractor carries the full working.

7Rate adjustment

Requirement 5.7 — rates are editable and costs recalculate. Collapsed by default to keep the estimate clean. Here one material rate was raised and the total moved from Rs 40,770 to Rs 3,97,777.

8Excluding a surface

Unticking a surface removes it from the estimate: the polygon dims, the material picker drops that component, and the total recalculates. This is requirement 5.2's "review and adjust" in its simplest usable form.

9Error states

An unusable photo is rejected before anything is estimated from it, with a specific reason and practical advice on retaking it — requirement 5.1 asks for exactly this rather than a generic failure.

10Responsive

The two-column viewer collapses to a single column below 900px, and the comparison grid stacks below 700px. Everything remains usable on a phone.

11The whole flow

All four stages on one continuous page, so you can scroll back to change a material and watch the total move without losing your place.
